\# Manifestation (Canonical Definition)



Manifestation is NOT:



\* appearance alone

\* character presentation

\* finalized form



Manifestation is temporary observable convergence.



\---



\## Structural Role



Manifestation occurs when:



\* convergence stabilizes temporarily

\* observation persists

\* structural contradiction remains unresolved



Manifestation should preserve:



\* incompleteness

\* ambiguity

\* instability



\---



\## Operational Layer



Manifestation may vary across:



\* phase.low

\* phase.mid

\* phase.high



Manifestation does not guarantee:



\* identity stability

\* narrative continuity

\* interpretational closure



\---



\## Operational Notes



Manifestation should not become:



\* fully resolved

\* emotionally directive

\* narratively over-explained
