\# Ambivalence (Canonical Definition)



Ambivalence is NOT:



\* mood

\* atmosphere

\* emotional confusion

\* aesthetic contradiction



Ambivalence is a structural condition.



\---



\## Structural Definition



Ambivalence describes:



\* simultaneous incompatible states

\* unresolved coexistence

\* non-finalized structural relation



Ambivalence may appear:



\* emotional

\* spatial

\* symbolic

\* observational



but should not be reduced to emotion alone.



\---



\## Topological Interpretation



Ambivalence may be interpreted as:



\* non-Hausdorff coexistence

\* unresolved boundary persistence

\* simultaneous incompatible continuity



\---



\## Operational Notes



Operational aesthetics must not replace

the structural definition of Ambivalence.
