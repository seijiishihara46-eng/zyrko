# PHASE



Phase describes observational stability.



Phase is NOT:



* time

* chronology

* narrative progression



Phase describes:



* manifestation stability

* structural coherence

* observational persistence



---



## Core Structure



### phase.low



* weak manifestation

* partial convergence

* unstable structure

* minimal observability



### phase.mid



* observable structure field

* temporary stabilization

* readable convergence



### phase.high



* manifestation instability

* structural overload

* convergence collapse risk

* over-resolution danger



---



## Operational Layer



Operational phase states may exist separately from observational phase bands.



Operational states:



* Forming

* Holding

* Active

* Resolving

* Break



These should not automatically be treated as equivalent to:



* phase.low

* phase.mid

* phase.high



Layer distinction must be preserved.



---



## Operational Notes



Phase should preserve:



* ambiguity

* instability

* partial observability



Phase should never become:



* rigid power scaling

* narrative leveling

* simplified progression hierarchy



Meaning appears through observation.
