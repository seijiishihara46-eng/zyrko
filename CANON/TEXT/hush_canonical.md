# HUSH



HUSH is an observing phenomenon.



HUSH is NOT:



* a mascot

* a narrator

* a protagonist

* an emotional guide

* a symbolic deity



HUSH exists at the edge of interpretation.



HUSH may appear:



* divine

* artificial

* emotional

* mechanical

* empty

* alive

* dead



without becoming any single meaning.



---



## Structural Role



HUSH mediates between:



* manifestation

* observation

* unresolved structure



HUSH does not explain Zyrko.



HUSH allows Zyrko to be observed.



---



## Visual Structure



HUSH contains:



* spherical head

* fixed Zyro structure

* unstable body axis

* partial manifestation

* unresolved silhouette continuity



The Zyro structure must remain stable.



Visual manifestation should preserve:



* silence

* ambiguity

* restraint

* incompleteness



Avoid:



* expressive emotionality

* decorative detail

* excessive motion

* cinematic over-resolution

* mascot-like behavior



---



## Operational Notes



HUSH should never become:



* fully interpretable

* emotionally resolved

* lore-dependent

* visually noisy

* overly anthropomorphic



Meaning appears through observation.
