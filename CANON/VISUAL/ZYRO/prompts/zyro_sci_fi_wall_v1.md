# Zyro Sci-Fi Wall Prompt v1

Status: active
Created: 2026-07-09
Use case: massive sci-fi wall / relief / embedded glyph environment

## Intent

Generate a scene where Zyro is embedded into a large dark futuristic wall. The wall can carry atmosphere and material complexity, but Zyro's internal visual law must remain stable.

## Prompt

```text
Create a wide cinematic sci-fi environment with a massive dark stone-and-metal wall dominating the scene. The wall feels ancient-future: industrial panels, worn stone, subtle scratches, cracks, vertical light slits, faint mist, wet floor reflections, and deep scale.

At the center of the wall, embed the canonical Zyro glyph as a carved relief or inset dark material. Zyro is not a newly designed logo. Preserve the black infinity-shaped field, one continuous luminous white Orbit channel, restrained left spiral, single convergence crossing, horizontal 2:1 structure, and an empty right Void chamber.

The right Void chamber must be a plain dark field enclosed only by the continuous Orbit path. Do not add a circular recess, circular depression, lens, eye, inner oval ring, crescent, partial inner line, extra arc, glowing orb, mechanical plate, decorative crack, or any extra symbol inside the right chamber.

The scene can be dramatic and cinematic, but the Zyro glyph must remain quiet, minimal, and controlled. High contrast, dark atmosphere, subtle white light from the Orbit, no colorful logo treatment, no extra marks inside the symbol.
```

## Negative Prompt Concepts

```text
round hole on right side, circular recess, eye, lens, moon crescent, inner ring, extra white mark, double orbit, decorative spiral, generic infinity logo, glowing orb, plate, medallion, mechanical disk, ornate logo, colorful symbol, filled right chamber detail
```

## Acceptance Criteria

- Zyro reads as embedded in wall, not floating sticker.
- Orbit is one continuous luminous channel.
- Right chamber is plain dark field.
- No circular pit, lens, eye, crescent, or inner mark appears on the right side.
- Wall detail supports the symbol, but does not invade the chamber.
