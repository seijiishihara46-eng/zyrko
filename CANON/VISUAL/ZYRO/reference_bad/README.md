## Known Failure Patterns

### right_crescent_line

Description:

- The right chamber contains a half-finished crescent, arc, or partial inner white line.

Why rejected:

- The right Void chamber must remain a plain dark field.

### right_circular_recess

Description:

- The right chamber becomes a circular hole, dish, lens, mechanical plate, or oval pit.

Why rejected:

- This turns the chamber into an object instead of negative space.

### right_eye_lens

Description:

- The right chamber reads as an eye, camera lens, glowing orb, or sensor.

Why rejected:

- Zyro should not become a sci-fi device icon unless separately canonized.

### orbit_multiple_lines

Description:

- The Orbit becomes multiple strokes, parallel channels, or decorative wiring.

Why rejected:

- Canonical Zyro uses one continuous Orbit.

### generic_infinity_logo

Description:

- The symbol becomes a generic infinity logo without the restrained left spiral and right chamber behavior.

Why rejected:

- Zyro must remain specific, not merely infinity-shaped.

## Rule

A bad reference is valuable only when the failure reason is clear enough to improve future prompts or QA.
